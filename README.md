A 3D low-poly flight game in three.js. Fly the twin-prop courier aircraft Petrel across a dawn archipelago while a storm front moves in: ring gates, supply drops, short-strip landings and survey photo passes across 8 sorties and 4 mission types. Fuel and damage change decisions, a storm wind system with gusts and turbulence must be ridden, and a beacon beam plus radar make the next objective findable at a glance.

## Play

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser (plain static files, no network requests), or serve the folder:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Then open http://localhost:8080/game/index.html.

W/S throttle, arrow keys pitch &amp; roll, A/D yaw, hold the mouse to steer, F flaps, Space brake, B drop a crate, C chase/cockpit camera, R reset to Kestrel Field, P pause, M mute, 1–8 pick a sortie, Enter start. Land on any strip at low speed to refuel and repair.
